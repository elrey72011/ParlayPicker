Published Pick Results

Locked results track the owner’s explicitly locked overall picks and their original lines and odds. Locking is not wager approval or proof of a placed bet. Other verified records use the first confirmed, fresh pregame publication per game and category. Imported research is a separate historical CSV recap; its publication and capture timing are unverified. Win rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses). Pushes, pending results and cases needing review are excluded. Categories overlap; do not add them together. Results are selection records, not verified betting returns.

Parlays count as complete tickets after every leg settles. A ticket with a losing leg is a loss; otherwise a pushed leg excludes the ticket from win percentage. Parlay prices are illustrative and tickets remain research. Historical CSVs appear only under Imported research. MLB props appear in the Player Props results view. Missing statistics and unverified appearances are labeled Needs review; DFS is excluded. Results update when the owner grades and republishes.
