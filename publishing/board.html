How to read this board

Current game picks prefer exact Novig quotes. College football, and NFL when its fallback is enabled, can use a verified DraftKings, FanDuel or BetMGM quote when Novig is unavailable; the sportsbook is shown for each pick. Quote times are provider timestamps; availability may change. Locked picks above preserve their original selections and prices.

High estimated win probability does not imply positive price value. Break-even is the saved price’s no-push equivalent; push-aware EV remains the producer estimate. Input health describes instrumented signals only, not verified weather or lineup coverage. Highest recorded model win estimate first; missing estimates appear last. This order does not establish accuracy or wager approval. Research combinations may include PASS selections and college sportsbook fallbacks. How results are recorded

Locked results track the owner’s explicitly locked overall picks and their original lines and odds. Locking is not wager approval or proof of a placed bet. Other verified records use the first confirmed, fresh pregame publication per game and category. Imported research is a separate historical CSV recap; its publication and capture timing are unverified. Win rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses). Pushes, pending results and cases needing review are excluded. Categories overlap; do not add them together. Results are selection records, not verified betting returns.
