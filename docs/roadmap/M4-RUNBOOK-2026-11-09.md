# M4 runbook: freeze, validate, review, activate

Written 2026-11-09 for `docs/roadmap/VALIDATION-ROADMAP-2026-11-09.md`, milestone M4. It is the
ordered list of steps from "the price watch has a bet rate" to "a pick is staked". Every step that
grants authority is the owner's. Nothing here is done by merging code.

M4 is mostly calendar time. Nothing in it can be hurried without weakening the test.

## Rules that hold through the windows

- Do not change the functions the plan hashes (`pinnacle_model.PREDICTION_FUNCTIONS`). A change stops
  the plan's predictions, visibly.
- Do not change a frozen plan. A different rule is a new plan version and new windows.
- Do not freeze one pooled scope without the other. A missing or differing companion blocks both.
- A scope that ends a window with fewer than 10 bets of its own fails, whatever the pool did.
- The capture that feeds the plan must keep running for both windows. The price watch stops by
  itself after 2026-11-16; continuing it is a new spend decision at the M2 review.

## What the first plan costs in time

Each window is sized as `min_bets / (bets per day × 0.8) × 1.25` days, at least 7.

| Bets a day (pool) | 75 bets a window | 100 bets a window |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 59 days each | 79 days each |
| 4 | 30 days each | 40 days each |
| 8 | 15 days each | 20 days each |

Two windows, so the total is twice the figure. At 2 bets a day and 75 bets, a plan frozen on
2026-11-17 ends its holdout in mid March 2027. The roadmap's "late December to mid January" needs
about 6 bets a day or more.

## Two plans: spreads and totals

Owner 2026-11-09: the M2 report covers a second pooled plan, totals across NBA and NCAAB, beside
the spreads plan.

- The two are separate plans. Spreads and totals are bets on the same games, so they are never
  pooled with each other; each family pools only across the two sports.
- Each is drafted, frozen, validated, reviewed and activated on its own. One failing does not
  affect the other.
- It costs no more to capture: the price watch already requests spreads and totals together.
- Open until the report: whether NBA and NCAAB totals each have a registered Pinnacle model and
  enough settled games for a calibration. Research capture has taken Pinnacle prices mainly for
  spreads.

## NHL in the pool

Owner 2026-11-09 (evening): add NHL to widen the net.

- Built: the price watch covers NHL puck lines and totals every 15 minutes; the replay in the
  readiness report includes NHL; a pool may name NHL. A pool is one kind of market across sports:
  totals with totals, or side markets together (NBA and NCAAB spreads with the NHL puck line).
- The pool's sports are chosen at the draft (`pool_sports`). Every sport named must freeze the
  same plan, so add NHL to a pool only once NHL can be drafted; otherwise it blocks the others.
- Not built, and needed before an NHL **plan** can be drafted: a registered NHL Pinnacle model.
  Registration follows the research loop's Pinnacle capture, which decision D4 limits to NBA and
  NCAAB with its own credit allowance. Extending D4 to NHL is a separate decision, worth making
  only if the replay shows NHL bets.
- Novig for NHL is captured only if the repository variable `PARLAYPICKER_NOVIG_SPORTS` names NHL.
  Without it the watch still captures Pinnacle, DraftKings, FanDuel and BetMGM.
- Allowance with three sports: 300 credits a day, 2,100 in all (fast mode: 900 and 6,300).

Moneylines are not built. They are a new market family for the evidence store, the plan
templates, settlement, the gate and the app's pick pipeline (the app has moneyline picks switched
off), not an extra market on a request. That is several days of work in the authority chain and
needs its own decision.
