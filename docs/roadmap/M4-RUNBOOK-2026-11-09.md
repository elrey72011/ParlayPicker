# M4 runbook: freeze, validate, review, activate

Written 2026-11-09 for `docs/roadmap/VALIDATION-ROADMAP-2026-11-09.md`, milestone M4. It is the
ordered list of steps from "the price watch has a bet rate" to "a pick is staked". Every step that
grants authority is the owner's. Nothing here is done by merging code.

M4 is mostly calendar time. Nothing in it can be hurried without weakening the test.

## Rules that hold through the windows

- Do not change the functions the plan hashes (`pinnacle_model.PREDICTION_FUNCTIONS`). A change stops
  the plan's predictions, visibly.
- Do not change a frozen plan. A different rule is a new plan version and new windows.
- Do not freeze one pooled scope without the other. A missing or differing companion blocks both.
- A scope that ends a window with fewer than 10 bets of its own fails, whatever the pool did.
- The capture that feeds the plan must keep running for both windows. The price watch stops by
  itself after 2026-11-16; continuing it is a new spend decision at the M2 review.

## What the first plan costs in time

Each window is sized as `min_bets / (bets per day × 0.8) × 1.25` days, at least 7.

| Bets a day (pool) | 75 bets a window | 100 bets a window |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 59 days each | 79 days each |
| 4 | 30 days each | 40 days each |
| 8 | 15 days each | 20 days each |

Two windows, so the total is twice the figure. At 2 bets a day and 75 bets, a plan frozen on
2026-11-17 ends its holdout in mid March 2027. The roadmap's "late December to mid January" needs
about 6 bets a day or more.
