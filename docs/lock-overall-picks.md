# Lock Overall Best Picks

1. Restore public history in Workspace > Preview & Publish.
2. Run Game Analysis when ready to choose today's picks, then build and review the preview.
3. In Lock Overall Best Picks, choose the games and click **Lock selected picks**. Only today's unstarted games with analysis no more than 15 minutes old qualify. The save checks freshness again when clicked.
4. Build a new preview, review it, and publish to your configured website.
5. In Results choose **Selections: Locked** to see the record for those exact overall picks. Grade picks for the selected date or let the configured game grading scheduler collect final scores, then rebuild and publish updated results.

Each game's first lock preserves its pick, line, odds and timestamp in Drive. Later analysis may change the live Game Picks board but cannot replace a lock. You may add other unstarted games later. Locks are separate from Research, Approved and Imported research; locking does not approve or place a wager. This action does not rewrite yesterday's history or support retrospective locks.

If a Drive save fails, restore history before retrying: some game locks may have saved. They remain first-write-wins. Existing locks appear below the preview for review. Back-to-back games with the same teams and date require disambiguation and cannot be locked as a combined board.
