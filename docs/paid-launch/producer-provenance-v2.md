# Versioned research producer provenance

This bounded successor carries genuine originating provider facts through the
score producer, candidate construction, capture, per-game export and saved
research display. It changes no probability formula, model weights, calibration,
qualification, price gate, allocation or stake. An available research estimate
can still be PASS with zero stake.

## Origin and identity

`provider-offer-facts-v1` records the source event namespace/ID, named home and
away teams, original start, exact bookmaker, selected market/side, signed line,
American price and market update time (bookmaker fallback remains unchanged).
The extractor copies `market.period` and `market.settlement_rules` only when
present, with their source paths. It supplies no period or settlement defaults.
No applicable bookmaker rule contract is established by this software change.

The real score predictor records `research-producer-v2` inside version-2
`ml_estimate_metadata`, after its explicit success/unavailable outcome. It binds
these offer facts and its original inference clock. Capture's run clock remains
separate. The raw model value remains `ml_probability`; the blended research
value remains a separate recorded candidate/export value. A raw-model success
does not attest to independent inference or qualification of the blend.

The originating score-distribution model uses full-game team scoring statistics.
Its version-2 contract declares `target_period=full_game` separately from the
quote's sourced period. Complete first-half or first-five-inning quotes still
reject as target mismatches. This model declaration cannot fill a missing quote
period or establish a bookmaker's settlement rules.

A supplied outcome `quote_id` is identified as provider-issued in its provider
namespace. Otherwise a complete event/offer identity permits a deterministic
`parlaypicker:offer:v1:<sha256>` identifier, explicitly `locally_derived`. Its
canonical hash binds event, orientation, offer, clock, period and rule facts.
It is not a provider-issued or executable order identifier. Missing event/clock
facts prevent derivation. Missing period, rules or source references still reject.

The matchup label is explicitly an unordered team pair and Eastern day. It
cannot assign home/away or selected side. The contract checks named orientation,
event namespace/ID, start, exact market/selection/line/price and all supplied
aliases independently. Unrelated labels, swapped teams/sides, ambiguous MLB city
aliases, duplicate exact quotes and same-day doubleheaders reject. Conflicting
row aliases are preserved for rejection rather than overwritten.

## Historical and owner boundaries

Version-1 metadata and replay interpretation remain unchanged. Reading an old
bundle never adds a local offer ID, missing period/rules, inference outcome or
updated probability. Original recorded ZIP/package/CSV files remain immutable.
Software correction does not make an incomplete historical estimate AVAILABLE.

Version-2 owner traces record exact missing/conflicting fields at the producer
contract and the saved display rejection. The public package receives the
validated research-display object, not private traces or original provider data.
The existing renderer suppresses populated legacy numeric fields when that
object rejects. Display metadata is never a wagering authority input.

## Offline validation and source dependencies

Sanitized supplied transport fixtures exercise the actual predictor and pipeline
copy helper, candidate constructor, terminal PASS gate, immutable capture,
per-game export, package validation/serialization, retained replay and renderer.
They cover NFL half points, MLB unordered labels, original clocks, raw/blended
separation, negative EV, missing facts, side/provider conflicts and ambiguity.
Existing integer-push, unsupported NHL, started-game, mapping/clock, integrity
and owner-download regressions remain required.

The source must still supply verified period and applicable settlement facts
(including overtime/extra innings and postponement/shortened-game treatment as
applicable), or a separately documented and approved adapter contract must bind
them. Source rights, accepted production readers, genuine scoped calibration,
qualification/authority and fresh executable positive conservative EV remain
independent dependencies. Nothing in this successor authorizes fitting,
acquisition, recovery, registration, activation, publication or wagering.

## Exact software scope

Schema-9 binds actual owner-merged main and its tree, the unchanged original
baseline, preceding home Run Line policy/guard, exact implementation paths/blobs
and tree, and immutable predecessor files. A subsequent policy-only seal pins
the implementation commit without circular hashes. Three prior home fixture
input reads reconstruct exact predecessor source; all prior assertions remain
unchanged. Workflow, subscriber, NCAAF/DFS, frozen version 10 and prior policy
protections remain intact. The new selector cannot fall back after a bad seal.
