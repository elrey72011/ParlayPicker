# Football evidence inventory

Use Diagnostics > Run Readiness Report > Prepare football evidence inventory after remote evidence has been restored. The download uses hash-verified saved snapshots and score revisions. It is read-only and grants no training, ranking, or wager authority.

The inventory chooses the first recorded pregame best pick per sport and matchup before considering outcomes. It rejects unknown or post-start capture times and conflicting first captures. Exact duplicate records collapse. NFL and NCAAF are separate; NCAAF classifications remain UNKNOWN unless explicitly recorded. Settled research counts require provider settlement metadata and an original bound quote. They are not certified training rows: event mapping, historical feature availability, calibration and chronological partitions still need validation.

Candidate readiness now includes the original selected and opposing prices, opposing source and market probability. Old snapshots can have missing fields; never reconstruct them from current prices.

Final match status distinguishes missing from conflicting final cards while retaining the existing fail-closed blocker. The September 20 Carolina–Atlanta export lacks the raw final frame necessary to determine which occurred. Its original outcome and selection must not be rewritten to repair a diagnostic warning.
