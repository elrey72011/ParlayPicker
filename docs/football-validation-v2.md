# Football validation V2: frozen research policy

This policy is for NFL spread, NFL total, NCAAF spread, and NCAAF total separately. V1 rows and wager gates remain unchanged. The supplied September 24 CSV exports are backtest candidate research. They contain one NFL and one NCAAF matchup, with no reproducible exact-scope calibration or historical replay evidence; they do not establish a prospective cohort.

## Statistical decision

V2 uses a **fixed checkpoint**, not optional stopping. A single confirmation check occurs after the frozen confirmation window closes. An untouched holdout is checked after its later window closes for possible standard review. A later policy revision is required for any other checkpoint. `UNVALIDATED` remains the state until an exact-scope model and calibration are registered, the confirmation evidence passes, an immutable validation artifact is made, and a separate review accepts it. Provisional review never authorizes a stake. Premium is unavailable under V2.

For each of four metrics in each of two cohorts, the simultaneous familywise error budget is 0.05/8. For `n` independent decided events, Brier and coverage use the two-sided Hoeffding radius `sqrt(log(16/0.05)/(2n))`. Log loss clips probabilities to `[0.01, 0.99]`, so its radius is `-log(0.01)` times that value. Ten-bin expected calibration error uses the same radius plus `sqrt(10/n)` to allow for finite-bin plug-in bias. These are conservative distribution-free bounds; they assume independent event outcomes conditional on frozen predictions and fixed bin definitions. No opposing sides, reprices, or books create a second event. Pushes and voids are refunds and do not enter decided scores; unresolved results block passage.

The predeclared maximum uncertainty allowances are 0.05 Brier, 0.10 log loss, 0.05 calibration error, and 0.05 coverage. A deterministic search for the first `n` satisfying all four yields **9,451 effective decided events for each exact market**. Equal minimums reflect the absence of defensible sport-specific variance, push, price-coverage, and dependence estimates. They are independent requirements, not pooled samples. If coverage is below 100% or games push/void, more raw games are needed. At the checkpoint, score upper bounds must be below V1's Brier 0.24, log loss 0.68, and calibration error 0.08 limits; the coverage lower bound must exceed 0.90. Exact verified entry price coverage remains 100%. Certified comparable close coverage remains 80%. Positive paper ROI does not substitute for proper-score evidence.

The NFL schedules 272 regular-season games over 18 weeks, with one bye per team ([NFL Football Operations](https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-schedule/making-the-schedule)). Even a complete full NFL regular season falls far below this conservative requirement. **NFL provisional validation cannot be achieved in the 2026 season under this V2 policy.** The same-sample NCAAF feasibility and its remaining-season capacity cannot be established until an authenticated complete schedule and conference/FBS/FCS audit exists. The current CFBD request is `classification=fbs`; FCS coverage is not established. The scheduler's two-hour due windows and limited NCAAF attempt cap do not prove full-slate coverage. Missing expected-games-per-week and price/settlement coverage remain unknown in the timeline rather than guessed.

## Chronology and replay

V2 plans freeze before any confirmation observation can count. The first eligible event and prediction must be after both the actual database freeze and the plan's UTC start. Existing 2026 games remain historical or research evidence. The historical replay gate requires stable provider event and team identity, exact line and timestamped price, quote at or before prediction and before start, as-of feature snapshot, reproducible model/runtime, training input availability before training cutoff, and result availability after prediction and start. A passing historical record is `REPLAYABLE_HISTORICAL`; it still contributes zero prospective events. Historical train/model-selection/calibration roles require a separate, explicit, nonconflicting assignment before use. Without an exact replay, prospective-only development is required.

A conservative per-pick probability requires a registered calibration interval under this policy and is the interval's lower bound. A cohort-level confidence interval cannot stand in for per-pick uncertainty. Missing interval or mismatch with the canonical prediction blocks V2. Certified closes must match event, market, selection, book, and line. Near-start candidates remain diagnostics.

## Operational boundary

The four V2 policy plans start unbound because no qualifying exact-scope model/calibration has been verified. A model binding requires an explicit newer plan before its future observations are used. Read-only inventory and timeline functions report local evidence and mark remote evidence unverified until authenticated restore succeeds. NFL full-season capacity is an upper bound; actual remaining-season capacity and NCAAF conference coverage require live provider audit. No V2 plan changes true-parlay leg gates, auto-activates a market, changes bankroll authority, or places a wager.
